# KYC and Account Opening Policy

Document ID: PD-09 | Version 2.8 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Compliance, Aurora National Bank

## Documents required to open an account
Every customer must complete Know Your Customer (KYC) before an account is activated.
- Proof of identity and address (any one Officially Valid Document): Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, driving licence or NREGA job card.
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) card, or Form 60 if the customer has no PAN.
- One recent passport-size photograph.
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar for OTP-based verification.

## Ways to complete KYC
1. In branch: submit documents and verification in person.
2. Video KYC: a live video call with a bank officer, available daily from 9:00 am to 9:00 pm through the Aurora Connect app.
3. Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC: accounts opened this way are limited to a total balance of ₹1,00,000 and must complete full KYC within 12 months, otherwise the account is frozen for further credits and debits.

## Re-KYC (periodic update)
KYC details must be refreshed every 10 years for low-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 2 years for high-risk customers. Customers receive SMS and email reminders and can update through the app, by post or at a branch.

## Nominee facility
Customers can add up to 4 nominees on a savings account, with the percentage share of each nominee specified. Nominees can be added or changed any time online or at a branch.

## Joint accounts
Joint accounts can have up to 4 holders. Operating modes are Either or Survivor, Former or Survivor, and Jointly. Either or Survivor is the default mode.

## Dormant and inoperative accounts
A savings account with no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months is classified as inoperative. To reactivate it, the customer must submit a request with KYC documents at the branch or through Video KYC; no reactivation fee is charged.

## Updating personal details
Change of mobile number, email address and communication address can be done through the app using OTP verification. Change of name needs a gazette notification or marriage certificate along with a branch visit.

## NRI accounts
NRE and NRO accounts are opened at a branch with a valid passport, visa or work permit, overseas address proof and PAN.

## Branch working hours
Branches are open Monday to Friday from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm and on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Saturdays from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm. Branches are closed on Sundays, the 2nd and 4th Saturdays and public holidays.
