# Savings Account Policy

Document ID: PD-01 | Version 4.2 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Retail Liabilities, Aurora National Bank

## Overview of savings products
Aurora National Bank offers five savings products: Regular Savings, Salary Plus, Senior Citizen Savings, Junior Savings (for minors) and Instant Digital Savings. All savings accounts are eligible for a RuPay or Visa debit card, net banking, the Aurora Connect mobile app and UPI.

## Minimum balance requirements
The Monthly Average Balance (MAB) depends on the product and the branch location.
| Product | Metro / Urban | Semi-urban | Rural |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular Savings | ₹5,000 | ₹2,500 | ₹1,000 |
| Salary Plus | Zero balance | Zero balance | Zero balance |
| Senior Citizen Savings | ₹2,500 | ₹2,500 | ₹1,000 |
| Junior Savings | ₹1,000 | ₹1,000 | ₹500 |
| Instant Digital Savings | Zero balance | Zero balance | Zero balance |

## Charges for non-maintenance of minimum balance
If the Monthly Average Balance falls below the required level in a quarter, a penalty of 5% of the shortfall is charged, subject to a minimum of ₹100 and a maximum of ₹600 per quarter, plus GST. No penalty is charged on Salary Plus, Instant Digital Savings or Basic Savings Bank Deposit accounts.

## Interest rates on savings accounts
Interest is calculated on the end-of-day balance and credited to the account every quarter, on the last day of March, June, September and December.
| Daily balance slab | Interest rate (per annum) |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹1,00,000 | 2.75% |
| Above ₹1,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000 | 3.00% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 3.50% |
Senior Citizen Savings account holders earn an additional 0.25% per annum on every slab.

## Salary Plus account
The Salary Plus account is a zero-balance account for employees of companies that have a salary-credit arrangement with the bank. Salary must be credited every month. If no salary is credited for three consecutive months, the account is converted to a Regular Savings account and the Regular Savings minimum balance rules apply. Benefits include 50 free cheque leaves per year, a free Platinum debit card and a pre-approved personal loan offer.

## Senior Citizen Savings account
Customers aged 60 years and above can open a Senior Citizen Savings account. Benefits: higher savings interest (additional 0.25%), additional 0.50% on fixed deposits, free doorstep banking for cash pick-up and delivery up to 4 visits per month, and a free passbook update service.

## Junior Savings account
A Junior Savings account can be opened for a minor below 18 years. For children below 10 years, the account is operated by the natural or legal guardian. A minor aged 10 years and above may operate the account independently with a daily withdrawal limit of ₹5,000. On turning 18, the customer must submit fresh KYC documents and a new specimen signature to convert the account into a regular savings account.

## Instant Digital Savings account
This account is opened fully online through the Aurora Connect app using Aadhaar-based Video KYC. It has zero minimum balance, a virtual debit card issued instantly and a physical card delivered within 7 working days.
