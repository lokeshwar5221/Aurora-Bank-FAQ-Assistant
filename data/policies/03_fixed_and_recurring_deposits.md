# Fixed Deposit and Recurring Deposit Policy

Document ID: PD-03 | Version 5.0 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Treasury and Deposits, Aurora National Bank

## Fixed deposit interest rates
Interest rates on domestic fixed deposits below ₹3 crore, effective from 1 April 2026:
| Tenor | General public (p.a.) | Senior citizens (p.a.) |
|---|---|---|
| 7 to 45 days | 3.50% | 4.00% |
| 46 to 90 days | 4.75% | 5.25% |
| 91 to 180 days | 5.50% | 6.00% |
| 181 to 364 days | 6.40% | 6.90% |
| 1 year to less than 2 years | 7.00% | 7.50% |
| 2 years to less than 3 years | 7.10% | 7.60% |
| 3 years to less than 5 years | 7.00% | 7.50% |
| 5 years to 10 years | 6.90% | 7.40% |
Senior citizens (60 years and above) receive an additional 0.50% per annum over the general rate.

## Minimum deposit and tenure
The minimum fixed deposit amount is ₹10,000 and the tenure ranges from 7 days to 10 years. Deposits can be opened online through net banking and the app, or at any branch.

## Interest payout options
Customers can choose Cumulative (interest paid at maturity, compounded quarterly), Quarterly payout or Monthly payout. For monthly and quarterly payout, the interest rate is discounted slightly to account for early payment of interest.

## Premature withdrawal
A fixed deposit can be closed before maturity. A penalty of 1.00% is deducted from the rate applicable for the period the deposit has remained with the bank. No interest is paid if the deposit is withdrawn before completing 7 days. Premature withdrawal is not permitted on Tax Saver fixed deposits.

## Tax Saver fixed deposit
The Tax Saver FD has a fixed tenor of 5 years with a lock-in period of 5 years. Deposits up to ₹1,50,000 in a financial year qualify for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, as per prevailing tax law. The interest rate is 6.90% per annum for all customers. Premature withdrawal and loans against this deposit are not allowed.

## Loan against fixed deposit
A loan or overdraft up to 90% of the deposit value can be taken against a fixed deposit at an interest rate 1.00% above the FD rate, without breaking the deposit.

## Auto-renewal and maturity
At maturity, the deposit is automatically renewed for the same tenor at the prevailing interest rate unless the customer chooses to receive the maturity amount. Customers can change this option up to 1 day before maturity.

## TDS on fixed deposit interest
Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) at 10% is deducted if the total interest paid in a financial year exceeds ₹40,000 for regular customers or ₹50,000 for senior citizens. If PAN is not provided, TDS is deducted at 20%. Customers whose total income is below the taxable limit can submit Form 15G (below 60 years) or Form 15H (senior citizens) at the start of the financial year to avoid TDS.

## Recurring deposit
A recurring deposit lets customers save a fixed amount every month. The minimum instalment is ₹500 per month and the tenor is from 6 months to 10 years. The interest rate is 6.50% per annum for general customers and 7.00% for senior citizens. A late payment penalty of ₹2 per ₹100 per month is charged on delayed instalments. If three consecutive instalments are missed, the recurring deposit is closed and the proceeds are paid to the customer's account.
