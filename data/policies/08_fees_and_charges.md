# Schedule of Fees and Service Charges

Document ID: PD-08 | Version 8.0 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Retail Banking Operations, Aurora National Bank

All charges are exclusive of GST, which is applied at the prevailing rate.

## Fund transfer charges
| Mode | Channel | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| UPI | App | Free |
| NEFT | Net banking / app | Free |
| NEFT | Branch | ₹5 up to ₹1,00,000; ₹15 above ₹1,00,000 |
| RTGS (minimum ₹2,00,000) | Net banking / app | ₹10 for ₹2 lakh to ₹5 lakh; ₹20 above ₹5 lakh |
| RTGS (minimum ₹2,00,000) | Branch | ₹25 for ₹2 lakh to ₹5 lakh; ₹50 above ₹5 lakh |
| IMPS | Net banking / app | Free up to ₹1,000; ₹5 for ₹1,001 to ₹1,00,000; ₹10 for ₹1,00,001 to ₹5,00,000 |

## ATM usage
Transactions at Aurora National Bank ATMs are unlimited and free. At other banks' ATMs, 5 transactions per month are free (3 per month in the metro cities of Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru and Hyderabad). After the free limit, ₹23 is charged per financial transaction and ₹8.50 per non-financial transaction such as a balance enquiry. If an ATM transaction fails but the account is debited, the amount is reversed automatically within 5 working days; a compensation of ₹100 per day is paid for any delay beyond that.

## Cheque and demand draft
- Cheque book: first 25 leaves per quarter are free; ₹3 per leaf after that.
- Stop payment instruction: ₹150 per cheque.
- Inward cheque return due to insufficient funds: ₹500 per instance.
- Outward cheque return: ₹150 per instance.
- Demand draft issue: 0.10% of the amount, minimum ₹50 and maximum ₹10,000.

## Debit card, statements and alerts
- Classic debit card: annual fee ₹200, waived if at least 25 transactions are done in a year. Platinum debit card: ₹500 per year.
- Debit card replacement: ₹300.
- Duplicate statement or passbook printing: ₹100 per request. Electronic statements are free.
- SMS alerts: ₹15 per quarter. Push notifications on the app are free.

## Branch cash transactions
Four cash deposits per month up to a total of ₹2,00,000 are free on savings accounts. Beyond this, ₹5 per ₹1,000 is charged, with a minimum of ₹50.

## Safe deposit lockers (subject to availability)
Annual rent: ₹1,500 for small, ₹3,500 for medium and ₹7,000 for large lockers. A refundable key deposit equal to 3 years of rent is taken.

## Account closure
Closing a savings account within 14 days of opening is free. Closure after 14 days and within 12 months of opening costs ₹500. No charge applies after 12 months.
