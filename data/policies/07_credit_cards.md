# Credit Card Policy

Document ID: PD-07 | Version 4.0 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Cards and Payments, Aurora National Bank

## Card variants
| Feature | Aurora Rise | Aurora Elite | Aurora Secure (against FD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joining / annual fee | ₹499 | ₹2,999 | ₹0 for the first year, then ₹250 |
| Annual fee waiver | On annual spend of ₹50,000 | On annual spend of ₹3,00,000 | Not applicable |
| Reward points | 2 points per ₹150 spent | 4 points per ₹150 spent | 1 point per ₹150 spent |
| Foreign currency markup | 3.5% | 2.0% | 3.5% |
| Minimum net monthly income | ₹25,000 | ₹75,000 | No income proof needed |
One reward point is worth ₹0.25 when redeemed against statement credit or the rewards catalogue.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years of age (Aurora Secure: 18 years and above), be Indian residents, and have a CIBIL score of 720 or above for Aurora Rise and Aurora Elite. Aurora Secure is issued against a fixed deposit of at least ₹25,000, with a credit limit of up to 90% of the deposit value and no credit score requirement.

## Interest and payment terms
- Interest on unpaid balance: 3.6% per month (43.2% per annum).
- Interest-free period: up to 50 days on purchases, if the full statement balance is paid by the due date.
- Minimum amount due: 5% of the total outstanding or ₹200, whichever is higher. Paying only the minimum amount due attracts interest on the remaining balance.
- Statement is generated monthly and the due date is 18 days after the statement date.

## Late payment fee
| Total amount due | Late payment fee |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹500 | Nil |
| ₹501 to ₹5,000 | ₹400 |
| ₹5,001 to ₹20,000 | ₹750 |
| Above ₹20,000 | ₹1,000 |

## Other charges
- Cash advance fee: 2.5% of the amount withdrawn, minimum ₹500. Interest at 3.6% per month applies from the date of withdrawal, with no interest-free period.
- Over-limit fee: 2.5% of the over-limit amount, minimum ₹500.
- Card replacement: ₹250.
- EMI conversion: purchases of ₹5,000 or more can be converted into EMIs of 3 to 24 months at 14% per annum, with a processing fee of ₹199 plus GST.

## Lost or stolen card
Block the card immediately through the Aurora Connect app, net banking or by calling 1800-120-4567 (24x7). The customer has zero liability for transactions made after the card is reported lost. A replacement card is dispatched within 5 working days.
