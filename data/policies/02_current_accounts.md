# Current Account Policy

Document ID: PD-02 | Version 3.1 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Business Banking, Aurora National Bank

## Who can open a current account
Current accounts are meant for proprietorships, partnership firms, companies, LLPs, trusts and societies that carry out business or professional activity. Individuals who are not engaged in business cannot open a current account. Salaried individuals should open a savings account instead.

## Types of current accounts and minimum balance
| Product | Monthly Average Balance (MAB) |
|---|---|
| Business Current Account | ₹25,000 |
| Premium Current Account | ₹1,00,000 |
| Startup Current Account | ₹10,000 (waived for the first 12 months for DPIIT-recognised startups) |
If the MAB is not maintained, a charge of ₹600 per month for Business Current and ₹1,500 per month for Premium Current is levied, plus GST.

## Interest
No interest is paid on balances in a current account.

## Cash deposit and transaction limits
Cash deposits up to ₹10,00,000 per month are free. Cash deposits above this limit are charged at 0.15% of the amount deposited, subject to a minimum of ₹150 per transaction. The first 50 free cheque leaves per quarter are provided on Business Current Accounts, and 100 leaves on Premium Current Accounts.

## Overdraft facility
Current account holders with at least 12 months of banking history can request an overdraft. An overdraft against a fixed deposit is available for up to 90% of the fixed deposit value at an interest rate 1.00% above the FD rate. Other overdraft limits are subject to credit assessment.

## Documents required
Business registration proof (GST certificate, Udyam registration, partnership deed or certificate of incorporation), PAN of the business, identity and address proof of all signatories, and two recent passport-size photographs of each signatory.
