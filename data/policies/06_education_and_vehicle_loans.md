# Education Loan and Vehicle Loan Policy

Document ID: PD-06 | Version 3.4 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Retail Assets, Aurora National Bank

## Education loan (Aurora Vidya)
Aurora Vidya finances higher studies in India and abroad for graduate, postgraduate, professional and diploma courses at recognised institutions.

### Loan amount and security
- Studies in India: up to ₹50,00,000.
- Studies abroad: up to ₹1,50,00,000.
- Loans up to ₹7,50,000 need no collateral. Loans above ₹7,50,000 require a co-applicant and tangible collateral such as property or a fixed deposit.
- A parent, guardian or spouse must be a co-applicant for all education loans.

### Interest rate and repayment
- Interest rate: 9.50% to 12.00% per annum (floating), depending on the course, institution and collateral.
- Moratorium: the course duration plus 12 months, or 6 months after getting a job, whichever is earlier. Simple interest is payable during the moratorium; paying it is optional but reduces the later EMI.
- Repayment tenure: up to 15 years after the moratorium ends.
- Interest paid on an education loan may be eligible for deduction under Section 80E for up to 8 years, as per prevailing tax law.

### Fees
Processing fee is nil for loans up to ₹7,50,000. For higher amounts it is 1% of the loan, with a maximum of ₹10,000, plus GST. No prepayment charges apply on education loans.

## Car loan (Aurora DriveEasy)
| Feature | New car | Used car (up to 5 years old) |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum finance | 90% of on-road price | 80% of valuation |
| Interest rate (p.a.) | 8.75% to 11.50% | 11.00% to 14.00% |
| Maximum tenure | 7 years | 5 years |
Processing fee is 1% of the loan amount, with a maximum of ₹10,000, plus GST. Foreclosure charge is 3% of the outstanding principal if the loan is closed within 12 months, and nil after that. Minimum age of the applicant is 21 years and minimum net annual income is ₹3,00,000.

## Two-wheeler loan (Aurora RideNow)
Finance up to 85% of the on-road price, at 9.99% to 14.50% per annum, for a tenure of up to 4 years. Processing fee is a flat ₹1,000 plus GST. Minimum net monthly income is ₹15,000.
