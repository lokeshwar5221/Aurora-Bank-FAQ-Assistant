# Personal Loan Policy (Aurora FlexiCash)

Document ID: PD-04 | Version 6.3 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Retail Assets, Aurora National Bank

## Loan amount and tenure
Aurora FlexiCash personal loans are unsecured loans from ₹50,000 up to ₹25,00,000, subject to a maximum of 24 times the applicant's net monthly salary. The repayment tenure is from 12 months to 72 months. The loan can be used for any lawful personal purpose such as medical expenses, travel, weddings or home renovation. It cannot be used for speculation, investment in shares or any illegal activity.

## Eligibility criteria for salaried applicants
- Age: 21 years to 60 years at the time of application, and not more than 65 years at loan maturity.
- Minimum net monthly income: ₹25,000 for applicants living in metro cities and ₹20,000 for other locations.
- Work experience: at least 1 year of total work experience, of which at least 6 months with the current employer.
- Credit score: a CIBIL score of 700 or above is required. Applicants with a score below 700 are not eligible for an unsecured personal loan.
- Total monthly EMI obligations including the proposed loan must not exceed 50% of net monthly income.
- The applicant must be an Indian resident with a salary account or a salary slip from a company approved by the bank.

## Eligibility criteria for self-employed applicants
Self-employed professionals and business owners must be between 25 and 65 years of age, have at least 3 years of business continuity, file Income Tax Returns for the last 2 years, and have a minimum annual income of ₹6,00,000 as per ITR. The CIBIL score requirement of 700 or above also applies.

## Interest rates
The interest rate is a fixed rate and depends on the applicant's credit score.
| CIBIL score | Interest rate (per annum) |
|---|---|
| 750 and above | 10.99% to 12.50% |
| 700 to 749 | 12.51% to 16.50% |
Existing salary account holders get a concession of 0.25% on the interest rate.

## Fees and charges
- Processing fee: 1.50% of the loan amount for salaried applicants and 2.00% for self-employed applicants, plus GST. The minimum processing fee is ₹1,500.
- Late EMI payment charge: 2% per month on the overdue amount, subject to a minimum of ₹500.
- Cheque / ECS bounce charge: ₹500 per instance.
- Loan statement and foreclosure letter: free once a year, ₹100 for each additional copy.

## Part-payment and foreclosure
Part-prepayment is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid, with a minimum part-payment of ₹10,000. Foreclosure charges on the outstanding principal are 4% if the loan is closed within 12 months of disbursal, 2% between 13 and 24 months, and nil after 24 months.

## Documents required
Salaried: identity and address proof, PAN card, latest 3 months' salary slips, and last 6 months' bank statement. Self-employed: identity and address proof, PAN card, last 2 years' ITR with computation of income, business proof and last 12 months' bank statement.

## Processing time
Pre-approved customers receive disbursal within 48 hours of completing documentation. For other applicants, the loan is typically sanctioned within 3 to 5 working days after submission of complete documents.
