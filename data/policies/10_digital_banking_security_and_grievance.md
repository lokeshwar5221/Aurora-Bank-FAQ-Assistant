# Digital Banking, Security and Grievance Redressal Policy

Document ID: PD-10 | Version 3.6 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Digital Channels and Customer Service, Aurora National Bank

## Aurora Connect app and net banking
Customers can register for net banking and the Aurora Connect mobile app using their debit card details and mobile OTP. The app is secured with device binding, a 6-digit MPIN and optional fingerprint or face unlock. A device change requires fresh OTP verification.

## UPI limits
- Maximum UPI payment limit: ₹1,00,000 per day per customer and 20 transactions per day.
- For payments to verified merchants in categories such as tax payments, hospitals, education and IPO subscriptions, the limit is ₹5,00,000 per day.
- Newly added payees above ₹25,000 are allowed only after a 24-hour cooling period.

## Reporting fraud and customer liability
Customers must report an unauthorised transaction as soon as they notice it, through the app, net banking, or by calling the 24x7 helpline 1800-120-4567.
| When reported | Customer liability |
|---|---|
| Within 3 working days of receiving the alert | Zero liability for third-party frauds |
| Between 4 and 7 working days | Limited liability of ₹5,000 for savings accounts and up to ₹25,000 for current accounts and cards with higher limits |
| After 7 working days | As per the bank board-approved policy |
The bank credits the disputed amount to the customer account within 10 working days of the report, after which the investigation continues.

## Safety guidelines
Aurora National Bank never asks for your PIN, CVV, OTP, MPIN or password by phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp. Never share these with anyone, including people who claim to be bank employees. Do not install remote-access apps at the request of unknown callers.

## Customer care
Customer care is available 24x7 at 1800-120-4567 (toll free) and care@auroranationalbank.example. Credit card helpline and lost card reporting are available on the same number.

## Grievance redressal process
| Level | Contact | Resolution time |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Branch manager or customer care | Within 7 working days |
| Level 2 | Nodal Officer, grievance@auroranationalbank.example | Within 14 working days |
| Level 3 | Principal Nodal Officer | Within 30 days of the original complaint |
If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days or the customer is not satisfied with the reply, the customer can approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman through the RBI complaint management portal at cms.rbi.org.in.

## Failed transactions
If money is debited but a UPI or IMPS transaction fails, the amount is automatically reversed within 5 working days. If the reversal is delayed, a compensation of ₹100 per day is paid to the customer.
