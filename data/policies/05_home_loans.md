# Home Loan Policy (Aurora HomeFirst)

Document ID: PD-05 | Version 7.1 | Effective from 1 April 2026 | Issued by: Retail Assets, Aurora National Bank

## Loan amount and tenure
Home loans are available from ₹5,00,000 up to ₹10,00,000,000 (₹10 crore) for purchase of a ready or under-construction house or flat, purchase of a plot and construction, home extension or renovation, and balance transfer of an existing home loan. The maximum tenure is 30 years for salaried applicants and 25 years for self-employed applicants. The age of the applicant at loan maturity must not exceed 70 years.

## Interest rates
Home loan interest rates are floating and are linked to the bank's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR), which is linked to the RBI repo rate. The rate range is 8.35% to 9.65% per annum depending on credit score.
| CIBIL score | Interest rate (per annum) |
|---|---|
| 800 and above | 8.35% |
| 750 to 799 | 8.60% |
| 725 to 749 | 8.95% |
| 700 to 724 | 9.65% |
Women borrowers (sole or first applicant) receive a concession of 0.05%. The rate is reset every quarter whenever the EBLR changes.

## Eligibility
- Age: 21 to 65 years at the time of application.
- Minimum net monthly income: ₹30,000 for salaried applicants. Self-employed applicants must show a minimum annual income of ₹4,00,000 in the latest ITR.
- Credit score: a CIBIL score of 700 or above is required.
- Total EMI obligations must not exceed 55% of net monthly income.
- Co-applicants such as a spouse or parents can be added to increase eligibility.

## Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio
The maximum loan the bank finances depends on the property value:
| Property value | Maximum loan-to-value |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹30,00,000 | 90% |
| Above ₹30,00,000 and up to ₹75,00,000 | 80% |
| Above ₹75,00,000 | 75% |
The remaining amount must be paid by the borrower as down payment (margin money).

## Fees and charges
- Processing fee: 0.50% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹5,000 and a maximum of ₹15,000, plus GST.
- Legal and technical valuation fee: between ₹3,000 and ₹7,500 depending on the property, payable at the time of application and non-refundable.
- Prepayment and foreclosure: no prepayment or foreclosure charges on floating-rate home loans taken by individual borrowers. The minimum part-prepayment amount is equal to one EMI.
- Interest rate switch or conversion fee: 0.25% of the outstanding principal.
- Late payment charge: 2% per month on the overdue EMI.

## Pre-EMI for under-construction property
For under-construction properties, the loan is disbursed in stages as per construction progress. Until full disbursal, the customer pays only the interest (pre-EMI) on the amount disbursed. EMI starts after the final disbursement, or the customer may opt for full EMI from the first disbursement.

## Tax benefits
Interest paid on a home loan may qualify for a deduction of up to ₹2,00,000 per year on a self-occupied property under Section 24(b), and principal repayment up to ₹1,50,000 under Section 80C, as per prevailing income tax law and regime chosen. Customers should consult a tax advisor. The bank provides an interest certificate every year free of cost.

## Balance transfer and top-up
Home loans from other banks can be transferred to Aurora National Bank. An additional top-up loan of up to ₹50,00,000 on an existing Aurora home loan is available at a rate 0.50% above the home loan rate.

## Documents required
Identity and address proof, PAN, last 3 months' salary slips and 6 months' bank statements (salaried) or last 3 years' ITR and balance sheets (self-employed), property documents including sale agreement, title deed and approved plan, and the builder's NOC for under-construction properties.
